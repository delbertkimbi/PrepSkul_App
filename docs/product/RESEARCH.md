# Research notes and source register

Reviewed 8 October 2026. These are primary product descriptions, official documentation and education evidence summaries. Product marketing is not independent proof of learning outcomes. The research does not establish that PrepSkul is the first or only product combining these ideas.

## Findings that influence the plan

|---|---|---|
| [Meta Muse](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/) | Persistent goals, background action, connected context and user control | Adopt inspectable preparation and scoped permissions; do not transfer productivity claims into educational efficacy |

## Competitor conclusions we can defend

There is overlap across diagnostic mapping, tutoring, progress visualisation and offline access. PrepSkul's proposed differentiation is a coherent local learning context plus continuity between AI support, independent evidence and human help. This is a strategic hypothesis to test with learners and tutors.

The earlier pasted chat contains a wider competitor matrix, including YourAIZone, Knova, Learningmap, Intellura, HSC Pal and PrepGraph. Its checkmarks and claims of an unmatched combination have **not** been treated as verified facts in this plan. A full competitive review would require current hands-on access, documented task comparisons and interviews. The supplied screenshots inform visual references; they do not establish product capabilities.

## Research still needed before implementation commitments

1. Confirm which Muse and Dots the user intended; current interpretation is the official Meta/OpenAI products above.
2. Observe the current app with real learners: where do they ask for help, quit, retry or misunderstand the route?
3. Recruit educators to select and review the first concept sequence and assessment items.
4. Audit actual curriculum coverage, rights and bilingual terminology—not merely available document counts.
5. Measure willingness to pay, tutor response capacity, mobile-money expectations and per-session costs.
6. Test voice comprehension, code-switching and math readout with consented local samples.
7. Compare map-first, home-plus-map and list-first prototypes on the same tasks.
8. Design an appropriately powered learning-outcomes evaluation with qualified researchers before efficacy claims.
9. Review child/guardian consent, data residency, safeguarding and voice rights per launch jurisdiction. This plan is a product specification, not legal advice.

## Source discipline

Record retrieval date, source version and limitations in curriculum ingestion. Preserve source attribution with generated teaching content. A public web page is not automatically licensed for bulk redistribution or model training. Model/provider capabilities and pricing should be rechecked at implementation time.
